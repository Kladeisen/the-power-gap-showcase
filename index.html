The missing link

National power is not local power

A country can look well supplied and still fail a data-centre timetable. The project exists to separate those two pictures.

Not enough

A national surplus

Annual electricity left over at country scale is not proof that a local data centre can connect.

What matters

Deliverable electricity

Power has to be available in the right place, in the required hours, and by the promised date.

How the research works

Four inspectable layers

Facts, calculations, scenarios, assessments and unknowns stay separate. If a generated view disagrees with the records, the view is wrong.

01 / Evidence

Primary sources first

Authorities, operators, regulators, permits and company disclosures, kept with provenance.

02 / Canonical records

One fact, one record

Human-readable, version-controlled facts with stable identities and explicit unknowns.

03 / Analysis

Rebuildable calculations

Reproducible work from those records and named scenarios — not copied figures.

04 / Publication

Generated views only

Maps, profiles and public pages are outputs. They never own the underlying facts.
